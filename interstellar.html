🎬Interstellar

  • Release Date: November 7, 2014
  • Runtime Duration: 169 Minutes (2h 49m)

  • Box Office Revenue: $731 Million USD
  • Production Budget: $165 Million USD

Plot Synopsis

In the near future, Earth is suffering from severe environmental problems and a global food shortage. Former NASA pilot Cooper lives with his children and works as a farmer, trying to provide for his family in a world where humanity's future is uncertain.

Cooper discovers a secret NASA facility led by Professor Brand. Scientists have found a wormhole near Saturn that could provide a path to potentially habitable planets in another galaxy. Cooper is recruited to pilot a spacecraft and lead a team on a mission to find a new home for humanity.

As Cooper and his team travel through the wormhole and explore distant worlds, they encounter extreme gravitational forces and difficult choices. Cooper must face the effects of time dilation and the emotional cost of leaving his family behind while fighting to save humanity.
