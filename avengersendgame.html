Plot Synopsis

Following the devastating events of Avengers: Infinity War, the surviving Avengers struggle to cope with the loss caused by Thanos and his snap. The universe has been left in ruins, and the heroes must find a way to undo the destruction.

Five years later, the Avengers are given a new opportunity when Scott Lang returns from the Quantum Realm with a possible way to travel through time. The team reunites and develops a plan to retrieve the Infinity Stones from different points in the past.

As the Avengers prepare for their final battle against Thanos, they must make difficult sacrifices to protect the universe. Their journey becomes a powerful conclusion to the stories of many of Earth's greatest heroes.
