🎬Parasite

  • Director: Bong Joon Ho
  • Writer: Bong Joon Ho, Han Jin Won
  • Release Date: May 30, 2019
  • Runtime Duration: 132 Minutes (2h 12m)
  • Original Language: Korean
  • Box Office Revenue: $258 Million USD
  • Production Budget: $11 Million USD

Plot Synopsis

Parasite follows the Kim family, a poor family living in a small semi-basement apartment in South Korea. They struggle to make ends meet by taking small jobs and searching for better opportunities.

Their lives change when the son, Ki-woo, gets an opportunity to work as an English tutor for the wealthy Park family. Soon, other members of the Kim family gradually find ways to work for the Parks by hiding their true relationships.

As the two families become increasingly connected, a shocking secret hidden inside the Park family's luxurious home is revealed. The situation quickly turns dangerous, exposing the deep divisions between wealth and poverty.
