Plot Synopsis

After the events of Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse, Miles Morales is now balancing his life as a teenager with his responsibilities as Spider-Man. He is still searching for a way to reunite with Gwen Stacy, the Spider-Woman who became one of his closest friends.

Miles eventually meets Gwen again and discovers that she is part of a team of Spider-People who travel across different dimensions. The group is led by Miguel O'Hara, also known as Spider-Man 2099, and their mission is to protect the Spider-Verse.

When Miles learns that his future may involve a tragic event, he refuses to accept the fate that others believe is necessary. He must find his own path while facing powerful enemies and confronting the consequences of changing the rules of the Spider-Verse.
