Plot Synopsis

Set in Gotham City, Batman continues his fight against organized crime with the help of Lieutenant James Gordonand District Attorney Harvey Dent. Together,they begin to make significant progress in cleaning up the city.

Their efforts are challenged by the arrival of the Joker, a mysterious and ruthless criminal mastermind who wants to create chaos and prove that Gotham's citizens are capable of abandoning their principles.

As Batman faces increasingly dangerous choices, Harvey Dent's transformation into Two-Face makes the battle even more personal. Batman must decide how much he is willing to sacrifice to protect Gotham and preserve the hoperepresented by Dent.
