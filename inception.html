Plot Synopsis

Dom Cobb is a highly skilled thief who specializes in extracting valuable secrets from people's minds while they are dreaming. His unique abilities have made him a wanted criminal and prevented him from returning to his children.

Cobb is offered a chance to have his criminal record erased and return home. Instead of stealing an idea, he is given an almost impossible task: planting an idea inside someone's subconscious. This process is known as "inception."

Cobb assembles a team of specialists and enters increasingly complex layers of dreams. As the mission progresses, the boundaries between dreams and reality become difficult to distinguish, while Cobb must confront memories from his own past.
