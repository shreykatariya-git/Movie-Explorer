Plot Synopsis

Oppenheimer follows the life of J. Robert Oppenheimer, a brilliant American theoretical physicist whose work played a major role in the development of the first atomic bomb during World War II.

Oppenheimer is recruited to lead the Manhattan Project at Los Alamos, New Mexico. Working alongside some of the world's greatest scientists, he faces the enormous challenge of creating a weapon before Nazi Germany can develop one.

After the successful Trinity Test and the use of atomic bombs against Japan, Oppenheimer becomes increasingly troubled by the consequences of his work. His political beliefs and connections later lead to a controversial security hearing that threatens his reputation and career.
